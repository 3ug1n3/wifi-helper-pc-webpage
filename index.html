What it does

  • Logs you in to utarwifi automatically
  • Reconnects after your laptop wakes from sleep
  • Can start with Windows and run quietly near the clock
  • Remembers your login, so you only type it once

Installing on Windows

  1. Download the setup file and open it.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This happens because the app is new, not because it's harmful.
  3. Click Next until it's done, then enter your UTAR login once.

Your password is safe

Your login is saved only on your own device and is sent only to the official UTAR WiFi login page. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
